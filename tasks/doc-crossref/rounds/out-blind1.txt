A contact's email address isn't shown as-is to the assistant — per the ContactOutput field table, the `email` field is obfuscated and replaced with a token in the form `{token}@obfuscated.invalid` before it ever reaches the AI. If you want to confirm this is actually happening, just ask the assistant to call `describe_obfuscation_policy`, which will report exactly what redaction is currently configured for that field.
